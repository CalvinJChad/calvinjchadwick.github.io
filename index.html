Hi. I'm Calvin.

I'm a programmer, DARE Academy 2026 Runner Up and a 4th Year BSc (Hons) Computer Game Applications Development student.

I’m a 4th Year student at Abertay University studying BSc (Hons) Computer Game Applications Development. I have a deep enthusiasm for various programming roles in games – allowing me to put my mathematics, programming and problem-solving capabilities into the creative field. I have a fondness for building deep gameplay systems with an attention to allowing ease of use for other designers. My passion for games covers a wide range of genres: from grand RPGs and exploration games to claustrophobic horror experiences. There isn't a genre of game I wouldn't be excited to undertake.

My passion for media doesn’t end at video games. My love for music had me learning to play guitar at a young age, which I continue to do. I have also delved into board games like Betrayal at House on the Hill as well as tabletop games like Warhammer 40k and have become enamoured by their complex systems. These passions have helped influence how I approach my technical and creative projects.
