About

Hi, I’m Calvin. I have a knack for wanting to learn and master any programming pipeline I can get my hand on. I have an intrinsic love for any creative form of expression and I’m always looking for ways to challenge my problem-solving skills. I have a breadth of genres in different mediums that I admire and that inspires my own creative work.
